**Short answer:** if the marks wipe off with a wet cloth, you need [window cleaning](/services/window-cleaning/). If white spots stay after the glass dries, those are mineral deposits and you need [hard water stain removal](/services/hard-water-stain-removal/).

## The quick test

Wet a small patch of the glass and wipe it dry with a clean cloth. Dust, pollen and fingerprints come off. Hard water spots stay put, usually as white rings or a cloudy haze low on the glass where sprinklers reach.

## Side by side

| | Window cleaning | Hard water stain removal |
|---|---|---|
| Fixes | Dust, pollen, fingerprints, salt film | White mineral spots and haze |
| Typical cause | Everyday dirt and weather | Sprinklers, hard water, runoff |
| How often | Two or three times a year for most homes | Once, then prevention |
| Comes back if | Time passes | Sprinklers keep hitting the glass |

## Which one should you book?

Most homes only need regular cleaning. Book stain removal when spots survive a normal clean, and ask about adjusting sprinklers so they don't come straight back.
